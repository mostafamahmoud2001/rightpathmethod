Common Questions About RightPath™ Certification

Is RightPath™ an accreditor?

No. RightPath™ is a learning-design certification and mark licensing body. It does not replace institutional, professional, regulatory, or programmatic accreditation.

Does RightPath™ certify subject-matter accuracy?

RightPath™ focuses on learning-design quality. Subject-matter accuracy remains the responsibility of the provider and any applicable academic, professional, or regulatory reviewers.

Does certification guarantee learner outcomes?

No. Certification indicates that materials were reviewed against RightPath™ standards. Learner outcomes depend on many factors, including learner effort, instructional delivery, prior knowledge, support, and assessment conditions.

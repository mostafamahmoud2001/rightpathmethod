Begin the Certification Process

Begin the RightPath™ certification process by submitting the application form and representative learning materials. The review team will confirm eligibility, define the certification scope, and evaluate the materials against the applicable RightPath™ standards.

Before You Apply

  • Identify the course, program, module, or assessment product to be reviewed.
  • Confirm the learner audience and intended learning outcomes.
  • Gather representative lessons, assessments, explanations, rubrics, visuals, and learner-facing instructions.
  • Identify whether you are applying for Reviewed, Aligned, Certified, or Certified with Distinction.
  • Prepare a primary contact for review questions and licensing coordination.

What Happens Next

  1. Application received and logged.
  2. Administrative eligibility check completed.
  3. Review scope confirmed in writing.
  4. Materials evaluated against the RightPath™ standards.
  5. Findings and score report issued.
  6. Certification decision delivered.
  7. Mark licensing agreement executed if approved.
  8. Directory listing published after final approval.

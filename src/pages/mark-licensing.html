Mark Licensing

Authorized Use of the RightPath Mark

The RightPath™ mark is a protected symbol of learning-design review. Organizations may display the mark only after approval, licensing, and verification of the certified scope.

Authorized Use

  • Course landing pages for certified courses
  • Program pages for certified programs
  • LMS course shells within the approved scope
  • Certificates of completion for approved programs
  • Catalog pages, brochures, and institutional marketing materials
  • Approved digital badges linked to the public registry

Required Mark Language

Approved Statement

This program is RightPath Certified™ for learning-design quality. Certification indicates that the program was reviewed against RightPath™ standards for clarity, reinforcement, active recall, feedback, and assessment-as-learning.

Mark Usage Rules

  • Use only the approved badge files provided by RightPath™ Institute.
  • Do not alter colors, proportions, text, seal structure, or surrounding clear space.
  • Do not imply accreditation, licensure, content endorsement, or guaranteed outcomes.
  • Display the certification level, scope, and renewal date where required.
  • Link digital use of the mark to the public registry entry when possible.
  • Remove the mark immediately if certification expires, is suspended, or is revoked.

RightPath™ Institute

About

Independent Learning Design Certification Body

RightPath™ Institute is an independent certification and licensing body for learning-design quality. The Institute reviews educational materials, training programs, and assessment experiences against evidence-informed standards for clarity, reinforcement, active recall, feedback, and learner progression.

RightPath™ does not replace institutional accreditation, subject-matter review, professional licensure, or regulatory approval. Instead, it provides a focused certification mark for the design of the learning experience itself.

Our Mission

Our mission is to help educational institutions, training providers, and course creators build learning experiences that teach while they test, reinforce while they evaluate, and support confident learner progress.

Our Role

  • Maintain the RightPath™ Learning Design Standards
  • Review submitted programs and materials against those standards
  • Grant approved organizations licensed use of the RightPath™ mark
  • Maintain a public directory of active certifications
  • Promote learner-centered, evidence-informed design practices

What We Certify

Eligible MaterialExample Scope
CoursesA single online, in-person, hybrid, or self-paced course
ProgramsA certificate, exam-prep sequence, continuing education track, or training pathway
Assessment MaterialsQuestion banks, practice exams, formative quizzes, skills checks, and embedded review tools
Learning ModulesFocused lessons, microlearning units, or competency-based modules

Independence and Integrity

RightPath™ certification is standards-based. Approval is granted only when submitted materials meet the applicable criteria. Consulting, remediation, and instructional support may be offered separately, but certification decisions remain tied to the published standards and review process.

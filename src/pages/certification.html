RightPath™ Institute

Certification

Review, Approval, and Recognition

RightPath™ certification is an earned recognition for educational materials that meet the Institute’s learning-design standards. Approved organizations may license and display the RightPath Certified™ mark for the approved scope and certification period.

Certification Levels

LevelMeaningExample Use
RightPath Reviewed™Materials have completed an initial review and received findings.Early-stage courses, pilot modules, or programs seeking feedback.
RightPath Aligned™Materials substantially follow RightPath™ design principles but may require limited improvement.Programs preparing for full certification.
RightPath Certified™Materials meet the required standards and may display the certification mark.Approved courses, programs, and assessment products.
RightPath Certified with Distinction™Materials exceed the standard and demonstrate strong learner-centered implementation.High-scoring programs with documented outcomes or mature quality practices.

Certification Process

  1. Submit application and representative materials.
  2. Complete administrative eligibility review.
  3. Undergo learning-design evaluation against the standards.
  4. Receive findings, score, and certification decision.
  5. Complete remediation if required.
  6. Execute mark licensing agreement.
  7. Appear in the public RightPath™ directory.
  8. Renew certification on the required cycle.

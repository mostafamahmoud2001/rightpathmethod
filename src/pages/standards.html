Learning Design Review Criteria

The RightPath™ Learning Design Standards define the review criteria used to evaluate submitted courses, modules, programs, and assessment experiences. These standards focus on the quality of the learning design rather than the prestige of the provider or the volume of content.

Standards of Excellence

StandardReview Focus
ClarityThe learner can identify the main point quickly and accurately.
Cognitive LoadThe material avoids unnecessary complexity, irrelevant wording, and confusing structure.
Active RecallThe learner is prompted to retrieve, explain, distinguish, or apply key knowledge.
Feedback QualityResponses provide meaningful explanation, not merely right/wrong scoring.
Foundational ReinforcementCore concepts are repeated strategically in varied and useful forms.
Assessment IntegrationQuestions and checks function as learning tools, not isolated judgment events.
Visual SupportVisuals clarify relationships, structure, sequence, or application.
Learner ProgressionThe pathway moves from foundation to application with appropriate pacing.
AccessibilityMaterials are designed for clarity, usability, and inclusive access.
Continuous ImprovementThe provider maintains review, update, and quality-improvement practices.

Review Criteria

Each submission receives a structured review using a weighted rubric. Scores are documented across required and recommended criteria. Certification may be granted, denied, or conditionally approved pending remediation.
